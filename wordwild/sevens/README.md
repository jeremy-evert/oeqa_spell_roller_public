# Super Sevens

Practice any times table from 1 to 20 (it opens on the 7s), each one 0 up to 20 (or the classic 0 to 12). The page is still called Super Sevens, and it renames itself: pick 8 and it becomes Super Eights. Static HTML/CSS/JS: no build, no dependencies, no network, no accounts. Scores stay on the device.

**Three looks** for the same game: Fire, Space and Candy. Each has its own background, floating decorations, confetti, and sound flavour. Pick one on the menu, or open `?theme=space` (also `fire`, `candy`) `?table=8` (any 1 to 20), and `?range=12` or `?range=20`.

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Practice** | Silly questions in random order. A clean first try is a star; a clue or a second try is a light bulb. A clue appears by itself after two wrong tries. |
| **Stopwatch** | A clock that counts up from the moment Start is pressed; best time is kept per range. |
| **Guess the Missing Number** | The same fact asked three ways, as a fact family: `7 × ? = 35`, `35 ÷ 7 = ?`, and `? ÷ 7 = 5`. Ten questions, a score, and the same two-step help. The rhyme is hidden here because it would give the answer away. |
| **Tricky Facts** | Spaced review. The game remembers, on this device, which facts were missed and which were known on the first try (five small levels, like Leitner boxes). Missed facts come first, known facts wait longer and then return. A round is up to ten facts. |
| **Count By** | Skip counting: a stretch of the count-by-t chant with one number hiding (`16 24 ? 40 48`, or backwards: `72 64 56 ?`). Ten questions, a score, the same two-step help, and the whole chant at the end to say out loud. It does not touch the Tricky Facts record. |
| **Build It** | Tap ➕ to stack rows of dots (tables 1 to 10 only; wider rows will not fit a phone), then count the dots and type the total. "Count with me" shows the running total and counts as help. |
| **Beat the clock** | Every fact before time runs out. 0 to 12: Bronze 90 s, Silver 75 s, Gold 60 s. 0 to 20: Bronze 150 s, Silver 120 s, Gold 90 s. The background turns yellow, then orange, then flashing red as time runs short. Medals and best times are remembered per table and per range. |

**Number pad:** the game has its own big on-screen keypad (0 to 9, backspace, and a green check), so the phone's keyboard never opens and cannot cover a button. A computer keyboard works too: digits, Backspace and Enter.
**The rhyme is printed up front** under each silly question, with the answer left blank ("7 times 3 is always fun. 7 times 3 is ___!"); it fills in when she gets it right. A setting on the menu turns it off.
**Help comes in two steps.** Tap 💡 once for a **Think** hint: a strategy that uses easy anchors (10s, 20s, doubles, the fact before) and never states the answer. For 7 x 17: "17 is 3 less than 20. 20 sevens is 140, and 3 sevens is 21." Tap again for **More help**: the rhyme or trick, a dot array (7 in a row, n rows) and a skip-counting strip, in a pop-up sheet. In the timed modes each step you ask for adds 5 seconds. In Practice, two wrong tries bring up the Think hint by themselves and four bring up the full help.
**Hidden surprises:** a few facts, streaks and secret taps do something special. Find them!
**Extras:** streak cheers at 3 in a row and every 5, confetti on right answers and a big finale, number animations, and three separate sound worlds with 16 synthesized effects each (no audio files). **Fire** is fireballs, crackling embers, brass and war drums. **Space** is asteroids: rock clacks, meteor fly-bys, impact thuds and a rumbling asteroid field. **Candy** is a bubble-gum barbershop: four-part quartet chords, gum-bubble inflate and pop, marimba keys and music-box bells. The menu links to a **Sound check** page (`soundcheck.html`) to hear every sound.

**Any table:** the 7s are hand-written (Jeremy's rhymes for 0 to 4, Claude's for 5 to 20). Every other table is generated by `factsOf(t)` in `facts.js`: a silly question from a list of 21 scenes, a template chant instead of a rhyme, a "Think" strategy built from 10s, 20s, doubles and halves, and a clue that spells the answer out with its last word in capitals. Tests check every one of the 420 facts: right answer, no answer in the hint, and every "10 8s is 80" claim true. Tricky Facts, medals and best times are kept apart for each table. For tables above 10 the help shows chips ("13 13 13…") instead of dots.

Files: `facts.js` (questions, clues, tables, ranges, medals), `engine.js` (logic and saving), `sounds.js`, `confetti.js`, `app.js`, `sevens.css`. Run `node --test tests/` here.

Honest limits: the clues are mnemonics written for fun and have not been reviewed by a teacher; the 0 to 20 medal times are a first guess scaled from the 0 to 12 times and may need tuning, and they are the same for every table even though 17 × 19 is harder than 2 × 3; the chants for tables other than 7 are templates, not poems; tested in desktop Chrome (headless) at phone width and by Jeremy on a phone.
