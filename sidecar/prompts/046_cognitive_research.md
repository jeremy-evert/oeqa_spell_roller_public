# Prompt 046: Research on cognitive decline, and a read of the games against it

**Status:** Research only. Nothing is built.
**Date:** November 17, 2026.
**Owner:** the project owner decides about any change; an AI assistant did the legwork.
**Source of the request:** the owner's words: "The games should also help with people struggling with cognitive decline. So we should research that as well."
**Mission file:** kept in a separate private coordination repository, not copied here. It asks for steps 1 and 2 of task T1 in `sidecar/TASKS.md` and nothing more.

## What was asked
1. Gather about 20 free, legal documents on cognitive stimulation and cognitive training for older adults and people with mild cognitive impairment or dementia (including what is *not* supported), on accessible and dementia-friendly game and interface design, and on caregiver-facing activity guidance. Record title, authors, year, URL, license, size, hash and a reason for each. Files stay on one local machine; they are not committed, uploaded or sent to a model.
2. Write a plain-language report: per topic, which sources cover it and a two-sentence takeaway each; what the research would and would not let us say.
3. Read each game against it (code and live pages) and say what fits and what works against this audience. Recommend a "calm mode" setting and estimate effort. **Do not build it.**
4. Update `sidecar/TASKS.md` honestly.

## Rules
- No medical claims. The games do not prevent, treat, slow or improve any condition unless a gathered source supports that exact claim, cited.
- No names or personal details of any person; no health information collected by the games, ever.
- Free and legal sources only; polite one-at-a-time requests; stop on 403/429; no long quotes.
- Do not ingest the documents anywhere. Branch and pull request only; never push to `main`.
