# Roll and Read: a phone spelling game

**Play it:** https://jeremy-evert.github.io/oeqa_spell_roller_public/

Open the link on a phone and play. Nothing to install, no account, and nothing is collected. Typed spelling and sentences stay on the phone and are never saved or sent anywhere.

- The game is a single file, `index.html`, made from a plain one-page worksheet. Every word and direction from the worksheet is kept exactly as written. Everything added is labeled NEW on the game's About screen.
- Voice is optional and starts off. A grown-up turns it on. On many phones, voice typing sends audio to the phone maker's cloud service, so it is not private or offline.
- Tip: use the browser's Share menu, then "Add to Home Screen", for a one-tap icon.

How this was made, and how to make one for your own document: https://github.com/jeremy-evert/oeqa
